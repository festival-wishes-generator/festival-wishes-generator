The Significance of Diwali

Diwali, the Hindu festival of lights, symbolizes the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. Families clean their homes, light traditional clay diyas, draw colorful rangolis, and exchange sweets and greetings.

Popular Diwali Greeting Examples

  • "May the divine light of Diwali spread peace, prosperity, happiness and good health in your life."
  • "Wishing you and your family a sparkling and joyous Diwali!"
  • "May this festival of lights illuminate your home and heart with endless joy."
