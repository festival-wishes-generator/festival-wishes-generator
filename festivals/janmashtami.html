Significance of Janmashtami

Krishna Janmashtami marks the auspicious birth anniversary of Lord Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu. Devotees observe fasts, sing devotional hymns, enact the Dahi Handi tradition, and welcome Kanha into their homes at midnight.

Janmashtami Greeting Examples

  • "Wishing you and your family divine blessings on this auspicious birthday of Kanha."
