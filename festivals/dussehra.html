Significance of Dussehra

Dussehra, also known as Vijayadashami, marks the victory of Lord Rama over the demon king Ravana, as well as Goddess Durga's triumph over Mahishasura. It signifies the ultimate victory of truth and righteousness over evil.

Dussehra Greeting Examples

  • "May this Dussehra bring success, happiness, and the destruction of all obstacles in your path."
  • "Wishing you a blessed Vijayadashami filled with courage and victory!"
