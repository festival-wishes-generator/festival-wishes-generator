Significance of Buddha Purnima

Buddha Purnima (Vesak) celebrates the birth, enlightenment (Nirvana), and passing away (Parinirvana) of Gautama Buddha. It is a time for reflection on compassion, non-violence, mindfulness, and inner peace.

Buddha Purnima Greeting Examples

  • "May the teachings of Lord Buddha guide you towards truth, compassion, and inner peace. Happy Buddha Purnima!"
  • "Wishing you a serene and mindful Vesak day."
