The Spirit of Holi

Holi is a joyous spring festival celebrated across South Asia and worldwide. It marks the arrival of spring, the end of winter, and the triumph of good over evil. People smear colored powders (gulal), water balloons, music, and festive sweets.

Holi Greeting Examples

  • "Wishing you and your loved ones a colorful, safe, and joyous Holi."
  • "Let's burn our negativity in the Holika fire and welcome a bright spring season."
