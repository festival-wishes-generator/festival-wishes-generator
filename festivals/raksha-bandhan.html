Significance of Raksha Bandhan

Raksha Bandhan celebrates the sacred bond between brothers and sisters. Sisters tie a sacred thread (rakhi) around their brothers' wrists as a symbol of love and prayers for their well-being, while brothers promise lifelong protection and support.

Raksha Bandhan Greeting Examples

  • "Happy Raksha Bandhan to my wonderful sibling! Thank you for always standing by my side."
  • "May our bond of love grow stronger with every passing year."
